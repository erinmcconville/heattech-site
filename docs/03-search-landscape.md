## 3. AI-answer research: what is and is not verified

The three requested questions were used as an analytical framework:

**"Who's a good heating engineer in Edinburgh for a new boiler?"**
A responsible answer should suggest a shortlist from independently vetted directories, verify live Gas Safe registration and relevant work categories, compare local experience and written quotes. Plausible cited sources include Edinburgh Trusted Trader, Which? Trusted Traders and Gas Safe Register.

**"I have storage heaters in an Edinburgh flat. Should I switch to a gas boiler, and what would it cost?"**
A responsible answer should not treat the cost of a combi boiler replacement as the price of first-time central heating installation. It should distinguish existing gas supply, new connection/meter, new wet radiators/pipes, boiler installation, flue and consent, insulation and possible heat-pump/high-retention-electric options. SGN says second-floor-or-above flats can require a bespoke connection quote. No reliable all-in Edinburgh price for storage-heater-to-gas conversion was verified.

**"How do I know a plumber in Edinburgh is trustworthy?"**
A responsible answer should explain Gas Safe verification for gas work, job-specific qualifications, independent reviews, Trading Standards/Which? vetting, insurance, scoped written quote and warranty documents.

**Not tested:** Independent live outputs or citations from Perplexity, Google AI Mode and Claude. Nor is this a controlled test of ChatGPT recommendations under location-personalised conditions. Do not report the publisher list above as "AI citations that all four tools used."

## 5. Verified Scottish funding, law and Edinburgh flue context

### Home Energy Scotland funding (checked 27 October 2026)

- Home Energy Scotland Grant and Loan is for eligible owner-occupiers of their only or main home, not ordinary buy-to-let landlords. Funding includes up to £7,500 grant plus an optional up to £7,500 interest-free loan for qualifying heat pumps or heat-network connections. Eligible insulation measures can attract up to 75% grant funding, subject to caps and scheme criteria.
- Ordinary gas boiler installation is not a qualifying clean-heating measure under this standard grant-and-loan offering. Some other schemes may support central heating, including gas systems, for specifically eligible households; do not advertise "£7,500 gas boiler grants."
- Home Energy Scotland describes a Private Rented Sector Landlord Loan, which is distinct from the homeowner scheme, and Warmer Homes Scotland, which can assist eligible low-income households with a range of measures, including central heating. Check current status with the relevant programme before making promises to prospects.

Official sources:
- https://www.homeenergyscotland.org/home-energy-scotland-grant-loan
- https://www.homeenergyscotland.org/find-funding
- https://www.mygov.scot/energy-saving-funding/warmer-homes-scotland-scheme

### Scottish gas boiler rules

- **Existing homes:** The Scottish Government stated on 18 November 2025 that introduction of its proposed Heat in Buildings Bill was being paused and that it intended to revisit legislation after the May 2026 election. This source is not proof that no further change has been made since; avoid turning older proposals (e.g. a 2030 date) into assertions of current law.
- **New-build properties:** The New Build Heat Standard continues to prohibit fossil-fuel gas and oil boilers as the main heating system in new buildings under its scope, while allowing some other forms of secondary heating. Do not confuse this with like-for-like replacement in an existing home.

Official sources:
- https://www.gov.scot/publications/heat-in-buildings-plans/
- https://www.gov.scot/publications/new-build-heat-standard-factsheet/

### Listed buildings, conservation areas and boiler flues in Edinburgh

- A flue penetrating or emerging through an external wall is an external alteration. For listed buildings, even relatively small external works may require listed building consent, and planning permission may also be needed. City of Edinburgh Council directs homeowners to check listed status and conservation-area designation and review its guidance before commencing work.
- Whether a specific non-listed Edinburgh flat needs planning permission depends on the building, proposal, elevation, designations and relevant permitted-development provisions. Do not apply the England-only Planning Portal flue rules directly to an Edinburgh tenement.
- For tenements, also assess flue terminal location, distances/ventilation requirements, roof/wall access and ownership/shared fabric permissions, with the surveyor/installer and council as appropriate.

Official sources:
- https://www.edinburgh.gov.uk/conservation-2/listed-buildings
- https://www.edinburgh.gov.uk/listedbuildingconsent
- https://consultationhub.edinburgh.gov.uk/sfc/conservation-adaptation2023/supporting_documents/Listed_Buildings_Guidance_14_October_2022.pdf

### Gas supply is a major conversion cost variable

- SGN offers a free check to determine whether a gas service pipe exists and can be used. A new gas connection may require further work and a supplier-installed meter.
- SGN publishes a standard Scottish new-connection estimate of £3,157 + VAT if SGN digs trenches (specific eligibility and physical assumptions apply); second-floor or higher flats can require non-standard assessments/quotes. That estimate is not a verified price for any particular Edinburgh tenement and excludes the new full radiator/boiler system.

Official sources:
- https://www.sgn.co.uk/gas-connections/household-customers/find-out-if-you-have-gas-supply
- https://www.sgn.co.uk/gas-connections/household-customers/apply-new-gas-connection0
