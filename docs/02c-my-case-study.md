# 02c — My case study: from electric heating to a hidden boiler in a period flat

**Source:** interview with the property owner (me), 27 October 2026. This is one customer's account, not independent evidence. Quotes are my words, lightly tidied from dictation.

---

## Case study draft

### The problem

I let out a tiny one-bedroom period flat in Edinburgh. It had an old hot water cylinder and electric heating that was only meant to run for a few hours a day. Guests would leave it on around the clock, especially on week-long stays. My energy bills ran at **£250 to £350 a month** for a really small flat, and I was getting constant complaints about the heating.

> "It was really horrible… I would just get so many complaints about the heating, and it was just really difficult to stay on top of things."

### The usual approach

For a few years, every time I asked for advice I was told the same thing: lift the floors to run pipes, and put a boiler where it would be seen. The flat has traditional Edinburgh wooden floors and not a single cupboard, so that meant exposed pipework and a chunky boiler in plain sight.

> "I really didn't want to do that."

### Why I trusted Ryan

Ryan already looked after the gas safety on both my properties. Two things earned my trust before the install came up:

- **He repaired instead of replacing.** He told me the boiler in my other flat would eventually need replacing, but that it had a good few years left. He knew I was planning to move and didn't have the cash for a replacement, so he did a small repair instead. The boiler is still fine.
- **He thought about my guests.** During a gas safety check he spotted that the fireplace could become a problem with guests using it, and advised disconnecting it. I hadn't even thought of that, and it has saved me a lot of hassle.

### What Ryan did instead

- **No floors lifted.** The flue goes out through the roof, and the pipes come down from the roof space.
- **The boiler is out of sight,** in the hall rather than the tiny kitchen.
- **A Hive smart thermostat**, which he suggested because he knew heating was my main guest complaint. I believe he has experience of renting out property himself.

### How the job went

- I decided at the last minute, and Ryan organised everything within **about one to two weeks**.
- He planned around the other renovation work going on in the flat.
- Ryan and Brett finished in **about three days** and left the place spotless.
- He was patient while I explored lots of options, and explained what I was paying for instead of just telling me not to worry about it.

> "He really took his time to walk me through everything."

### The result

- **Bills:** my energy provider estimates my annual bills will fall by **35% to 50%**. *(Forecast only: the install is very recent. Replace with actual bills after a full winter.)*
- **Guests:** no heating complaints since the install. *(Short period so far.)*
- **Control:** I can manage the heating remotely between and during stays.

> "I feel so much more in control with renting out my property."

### In one line

> "Ryan is just really easy to communicate with. He's very honest and sound. He's also really good at his job. He doesn't leave a mess. And he knows how Edinburgh flats work."
