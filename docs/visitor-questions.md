# Visitor questions

Oct 28, 2026 · working draft for the site review

The questions a visitor needs answered before they enquire, ranked by how strong the evidence is. Every section of the site should answer one of these, and no question should be answered twice (proposed rule; see decisions.md, 28 Oct 2026, for the design intent).

The **Evidence** column cites the research files. The **How the site could answer it** column is a proposal, not a decision. It favours proof and visuals over copy.

**Strength:** strong = several independent sources agree. Medium = one good source, or several weak ones. Weak = mostly assumption or competitors' copy.

## Gaps this list can't close without Ryan

- Prices and reply times (questions 1, 4, 10).
- Which jobs he wants more of (5).
- Aftercare (13).
- Photos (7, 9). Everything else can be answered from the evidence already collected.

## Gaps that need more research, not Ryan

- Google results from Edinburgh, Google Maps and "People also ask" (06 caveats).
- Answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews (06 caveats).
- The full text of the 12 cut-off reviews (05).
