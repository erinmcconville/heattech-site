## 3. Technical terms: definitions and source status

Reference definitions for terms in Ryan's posts. **CHECKED** = verified against the named official or manufacturer source on 27 Oct 2026. **GENERAL** = standard trade knowledge, no source checked.

| Term | Definition | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Unsafe appliance ("condemned") | When an engineer finds a gas appliance or installation "at risk" or "immediately dangerous", both carry the same warning label: "Danger Do Not Use". It's illegal to use a gas appliance you suspect is unsafe. Engineers may only turn off or isolate appliances with the customer's permission. "Condemned" is an informal term, not an official category. | CHECKED: HSE domestic gas FAQs, https://www.hse.gov.uk/Gas/domestic/faqs.htm; HSE OC 440/37, https://www.hse.gov.uk/foi/internalops/ocs/400-499/440_37.htm |
| Condensate pipe | A plastic pipe that carries water produced by a condensing boiler to a drain or soakaway. If it runs outside, prolonged freezing weather can freeze it and affect the boiler's operation. Insulating external pipework reduces the risk. | CHECKED: Worcester Bosch, https://www.worcester-bosch.co.uk/heating-advice/frozen-condensate |
| Condensate pipework upgrade | Changing the existing drain pipe when it's unsuitable for the new boiler. | Ryan's caveat (21 Aug 2025); no further detail checked |
| Gas pipe upgrade | Replacing a gas supply pipe that's too small for the new boiler. | GENERAL + Ryan's caveat |
| System flush | Cleaning sludge and debris out of existing radiators and pipes. | GENERAL |
| Chemical cleanse | A flush using cleaning chemicals. | GENERAL |
| Magnetic filter | A fitting on the heating pipework that catches magnetic debris circulating in the system. | GENERAL |
| Smart controls | Heating controls that can be adjusted from a phone, including remotely. | GENERAL |
| Manufacturer warranty | Manufacturer covers failed parts for a set period, subject to conditions. | GENERAL; Ryan's post links servicing to keeping it valid |
| Combi boiler | Heats radiators and provides hot water directly from the mains, with no tanks or hot water cylinder. | GENERAL |
| System boiler | Heats radiators and a separate hot water cylinder, with no loft tanks. | GENERAL |
| Mains pressure conversion | Removing loft tanks so water is supplied directly from the mains. | GENERAL |
| Back boiler | An older type of boiler fitted behind a gas fire in a fireplace. | GENERAL |
| Boiler relocation | Moving the boiler to a different position, often with a new flue position. | GENERAL |
| Flue | The pipe that carries exhaust gases from the boiler to outside. | GENERAL |
