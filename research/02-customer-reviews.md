# 02 — What Edinburgh customers say about heating engineers

Evidence from about 80 Edinburgh review texts and two forum threads. **None of these reviews are about Heattech.** They describe other businesses.

## Method and sample

OBSERVED
- **About 80 review texts across 12 Edinburgh businesses.** Every review with written text visible on a fetched page or in a search result was coded.
  - **Checkatrade:** The Heating Guy (Edinburgh), 7 reviews, Mar–Oct 2026; Versatile Heating Services, 8, Jun 2025–Oct 2026; Blue Flame Gas Services, 4 (2026, month/year partly unclear); Domestic Gas Services, 3 (2024); CHAPS, about 3 (2024–2025); one electric-heating review from the Edinburgh electric-boiler category.
  - **Trustpilot:** CHAPS Heating & Plumbing, about 17, Dec 2022–Mar 2026; Edinburgh Heat Team, 10, Jul 2021–Nov 2025; The Edinburgh Boiler Company, 4 snippets (2026, from a profile with 2,826 reviews and 4.9/5 TrustScore, fetched Oct 2026; 1-star = 24 / about 1%, not re-checked).
  - **MyBuilder:** City Boiler Care, 5 with text (Oct 2024–Jan 2026); Corstorphine Gas Services, 10 (Jul–Dec 2020); JM Gas Services, about 5 (2019–2020); The Edinburgh Boiler Company, 3 (2016–2018); one category-page snippet (May 2024).
  - **Yelp:** one Edinburgh plumbing review (undated).
- **Forums:** CityCyclingEdinburgh Forum, "Central Heating in flats" (18 posts, March 2019, read in full); Singletrack World, "Edinburgh plumbers/central heating engineers recommendations?" (search snippet only, undated, closed).
- **Reddit:** no r/Edinburgh threads could be retrieved.
- **Google reviews:** not directly accessible. A few appear second-hand as testimonials on installer websites (vendor-selected).
- **Official sources:** City of Edinburgh Council (listed-building/conservation guidance; householder guidance Sept 2025; Heat with Rent page); Home Energy Scotland (grant/loan pages and terms); The Gazette (listed-building consent notices).
- **Coding:** each review was tagged for every theme it mentioned, so one review can count toward several themes. Counts are approximate (±2–3) because some texts were truncated.

CAVEATS
- **Positive skew:** nearly every review is 5-star (CHAPS on Trustpilot: 100% 5-star). Complaints are badly under-sampled; complaint rankings are directional only.
- **Job-type skew:** mostly servicing and repairs, not installations. Install-specific and storage-heater customer voice is thin.
- **Age:** MyBuilder reviews for Corstorphine, JM Gas and The Edinburgh Boiler Company date from 2016–2020; the forum thread from 2019. Pricing and grant comments from that period are OUT OF DATE.

OBSERVED: wording and patterns
- Price praise uses words like "reasonable", "very fair", "well-priced", "good value". No review praises being cheapest.
- Many reviewers have used the same firm for 3–8 years, often starting with servicing (e.g. "We have used Chaps since Ian installed our boiler 8 years ago", Checkatrade, Dec 2024).
- Several reviews name the engineer personally (Ian, Steven, Kris, Lewis, Jack).
- Several reviewers describe repair-vs-replace decisions on old boilers (Versatile, Oct 2025: "the diagnosis for my decrepit old boiler was terminal"; Edinburgh Heat Team, Feb 2025: "They valiantly fought to keep an ancient boiler going").

## 3. What affected booking decisions

OBSERVED
- Fear of damage to a new kitchen delayed a boiler replacement (Edinburgh Heat Team, Trustpilot, Feb 2025).
- A firm was chosen after fixing rather than replacing a boiler the customer expected to be told to replace (Edinburgh Heat Team, Apr 2024).
- In an emergency, the first to reply won: "CHAPS were the one business who thankfully came back to me almost straight away" (Trustpilot, Aug 2023).
- No-pressure diagnosis: Versatile came "same day… gave me a ballpark quote… and, agreeing it wasn't worth the cost, went ahead and made the appliance safe while I consider next steps, all for the price of their standard call out" (Checkatrade, Oct 2025).
- Quote speed: "Quick response for a quote" (Heating Guy, Jun 2026); "Provided a cost for the work… organised a date and time that was suitable" (Corstorphine, MyBuilder, 2020).
- Comparing quotes and referrals: the Singletrack opener wanted "a selection of quotes/estimates"; replies were personal recommendations ("Used him several times. Top bloke.").
- Platforms as discovery: "requested through checkatrade" (Heating Guy, Aug 2026); "I found Ian on Checkatrade" (CHAPS, May 2025).
- Electric heating as a barrier to buying a flat: a 2019 forum poster had "previously been put off by flats with electric heating". Reply: "Check there is a gas supply in the flat before you begin – getting a brand new supply installed is rather long-winded" (CityCyclingEdinburgh, Mar 2019).

## 5. Running costs and savings

OBSERVED
- Energy-bill savings: 0 of about 80 reviews.
- Forum (CityCyclingEdinburgh, 2019): running cost appears in about 5 of 18 posts, about electric vs gas rather than hiring anyone: "the bills will be a lot higher than gas"; old storage heaters "were like burning fivers for heat"; a modern storage heater owner reported "about £55/mo" (OUT OF DATE). Carbon and fuel choice took up a similar share.
- Bill-saving testimonials appear on an installer's own site (Home Renewables Scotland, "Google review" quotes such as "Our bills have dropped"). Vendor-selected and bundled with solar/battery.
- Home Energy Scotland heat pumps page: in a gas-heated semi-detached home, a medium-performing heat pump "could save around £80 a year", a high-performing one £170; removing the gas supply saves standing charges of "around £128 over the year" (January 2026 figures).
- City of Edinburgh Council Heat with Rent page: storage-heater tenants' average all-in energy costs rise to £2,024/yr for a larger flat after the heating-charge change.

## 6. Edinburgh-specific facts

OBSERVED: customer voice
- Reviews almost never mention tenements, stairs, parking or flues. Edinburgh-related items: a Fringe-time emergency (CHAPS, Aug 2023); landlords and tenants ("caused by a tenant"); gas safety certificates for property sales (Edinburgh Heat Team, Sep 2025; a Checkatrade service "ahead of a property sale"); inherited "ancient" boilers; "unusual fittings" (Heating Guy, Jul 2026).
- Forum (2019, OUT OF DATE prices): gas supply questions in tenement flats; central heating in a 1-bed tenement flat costs "thousands, but not tens of thousands"; one full system with lead-pipe removal at "4500 in 2013".

OBSERVED: official sources
- **City of Edinburgh Council, Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas guidance (Oct 2022):** "Balanced flues will not normally be acceptable on the front or conspicuous elevations of listed buildings"; the flue "should be painted to match the colour of the surrounding stonework"; holes should be "formed with a core cutter"; "Listed building consent is required to install balanced flues on the front or any conspicuous elevation of listed buildings." CAVEAT: hosted on a 2023 consultation site; may have been revised.
- **CEC Guidance for Householders (Sept 2025):** "Flues on Listed buildings will likely require listed building consent." Edinburgh is a smoke control area.
- **The Gazette:** CEC listed-building-consent applications such as "Install gas service pipe… plus 4 boiler flues all to the rear elevation" (Canongate, 2014).
- **Radio teleswitch (RTS) storage heating:** CEC Heat with Rent page says RTS timers "are being switched off in June". Ofgem: "The phase out of the Radio Teleswitch Signal started on 30 June 2025". EDF Energy: the RTS signal "came to an end across all areas in the UK on 30 June 2026". CEC: "From 2 March 2025, Heat with Rent (HWR) payments will end."
- **Home Energy Scotland funding:** gas boilers and connections "are therefore no longer available to receive funding" since 30 November 2022. Heat pumps: up to £7,500 grant plus up to £7,500 optional interest-free loan. High heat retention storage heaters: fundable (£8,000 max, grant plus loan) only with "evidence as to why a heat pump isn't suitable". Warmer Homes Scotland (means-tested) can still include a "new gas boiler". RE-CHECK all funding facts on the day of publishing.
- **Competitor positioning (search results, not customer evidence):** Edinburgh installers ranking for "boiler installation Edinburgh" mention tenement flue siting, condensate routing, shared stairs, parking, "weight matters if you're up a tenement stair" (The Edinburgh Boiler Company), and fixed prices. Checkatrade's Edinburgh category page: average gas boiler install £2,481 (typically £1,941–£3,191); methodology unknown.
