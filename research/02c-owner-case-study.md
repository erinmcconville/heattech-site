# 02c — Owner case study: electric heating to a hidden boiler in a period flat

**Source:** interview with the property owner (the person building this site), 27 Oct 2026. Quotes are the owner's words, lightly tidied from dictation.
**Status:** one customer's account, not independent evidence. Treated as one hypothesis, not a conclusion. **Not confirmed by Ryan:** the technical description, Brett's role, and whether Ryan is happy for this job to be featured.

## Facts (owner's account)

OBSERVED

**Before**
- Tiny one-bedroom period flat in Edinburgh, let to short-stay guests.
- Old hot water cylinder and electric heating designed to run a few hours a day. Guests often left it on around the clock, especially on week-long stays.
- Energy bills £250–£350 a month.
- Frequent guest complaints about the heating.
- Traditional Edinburgh wooden floors; no cupboards.
- Advice from others over several years: lift the floors to run pipes, and fit a boiler in a visible position.

**Prior relationship with Ryan**
- Ryan already did the gas safety work on both of the owner's properties.
- On the owner's other flat, he said the boiler would eventually need replacing but had a good few years left. Knowing the owner planned to move and was short of cash, he did a small repair instead. That boiler is still working.
- During a gas safety check, he flagged that the fireplace could become a problem with guests using it, and advised disconnecting it.

**The job**
- No floors lifted. Flue out through the roof; pipes run down from the roof space.
- Boiler in the hall, not the kitchen.
- Hive smart thermostat, suggested by Ryan because heating was the main guest complaint.
- The owner decided at the last minute; Ryan organised the job within about 1–2 weeks.
- Planned around other renovation work in the flat.
- Ryan and Brett completed it in about 3 days and left the flat spotless.
- Ryan was patient while the owner explored options, and explained what the owner was paying for.

**After**
- Energy provider estimates annual bills will fall 35–50%. CAVEAT: a forecast only; install is very recent. No actual bills yet.
- No heating complaints from guests since the install. CAVEAT: short period.
- Heating can be controlled remotely between and during stays.

ASSUMPTION: "I believe he has experience of renting out property himself" is the owner's belief, not a confirmed fact.
