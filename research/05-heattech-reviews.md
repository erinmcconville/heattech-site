## What this means

INTERPRETED
- **Speed is what Ryan's customers mention most** (about 17 of 42), the same as across Edinburgh (about 40% in `02-customer-reviews.md`). His reviews also carry specific times ("within 5 hours", "within 30 minutes"), which are more concrete than any claim the site could make.
- **Repeat custom is the second theme** (about 14). This fits a business that grows by word of mouth.
- **Honest advice shows up as choices and sizing**, not as "repair, don't replace". That supports the decision not to make repair-first a theme.
- **Partial reviews hold some of the strongest facts** (Anya, Ebru, Samuel, Kate, Callum B, j n adams). Pasting their full text from Google would add to the proof available.
- **No review names an Edinburgh area or building type** apart from "flat", "2 bed flat" and "older house". Local relevance has to come from photos and the Stockbridge job, not from reviews.
